### BLOCK 4 — Cross-manual notes

- Coffee Buttons (Espresso & Lungo) are named as one label in the overview but split into two rows (Espresso button, Lungo button) because each has distinct procedures and press semantics.
- Water Tank & Lid is one overview label, split into Water tank (removable) and Water tank lid (hinged).
- Coffee machine row hosts the internal operating mode (cause); LEDs are the only observable effect (flat, integrated in the buttons) and are the natural ITS checkpoint anchor.
- Same button combination has different meanings depending on press duration and machine mode: both buttons short press = turn OFF (also entry of emptying mode, step 1); both buttons 5 s with machine ON = enter/exit descaling mode; both buttons 5 s after emptying setup = start emptying. Espresso 5 s with machine off = Auto Off setting; Lungo 5 s with machine off = factory reset; Lungo 5 s in setting mode = exit. Requires guards on the machine mode.
- Hold interactions (press-and-hold 5 s, press-and-hold until desired volume in Programming the water volume) are harder to model discretely; Coffee preparation (lever, capsule, cup, single button press) is the cleanest primary task for MR.
- Turn machine ON (press either button) and Turn machine OFF (press both buttons) are procedures of their own: Turn machine ON is a prerequisite procedure of Descaling and Emptying the system (both assume the machine is on without a switch-on step); the same switch-on / switch-off transitions appear inline as explicit steps in First use, Coffee preparation and Programming the water volume.
- Capsule ejection (lever lift-and-close) recurs in Coffee preparation step 5 and Descaling step 1: shared transition with a side effect on Capsule and Capsule container.
- Plugging in (First use step 1) and Disconnect have low ecological validity for MR: treat as precondition.
- Error model: the device emits no codes or messages; outputs are LED patterns only (blinking, steady, fast 3x, single/double, both, alternating, irregular). Troubleshooting symptoms are listed as observable conditions; all other entries labelled Inferred guard are derived from CAUTION/safeguard text, not device outputs.
- Internal parts (Blades, Extraction system) and flat markings (0.6 L line, Rating plate) are candidates for scene_props / excluded_components, not EFSM machines.
- Cup support (safeguards: ensure the cup support is in place) is not named in the overview; ES/BR text equates it with drip tray and grid. Listed as an inferred alias of Drip grid.
- Not tabulated (neither device_component nor material): User manual and Welcome to Nespresso folder (packaging documentation), power outlet, fuse and extension cord (external infrastructure), handles or knobs (generic safeguard boilerplate; the device has none).
- Manual inconsistencies: ES descaling step 4 states min. 0.8 l container vs 1 L in EN/FR and elsewhere; ES descaling step 8 says repeat steps 4 to 7 vs steps 4 and 7 in EN/FR/BR; ES energy saving step 1 says machine turning off vs turned off in EN; energy-saving icon artwork appears to read 3 min while text states 9 min (text taken as authoritative); energy-saving step 2 describes a toggle (first press 30 min, next press 9 min) whose blink feedback is single = 9 min, double = 30 min.
- Direction of removal for Water tank, Capsule container and drip parts is not specified in manual; layout hints for these must come from the exploded overview figure and be marked as estimates.
- Descaling frequency table: hard water (36 fH / 20 dH / 360 mg/l CaCO3) after 300 cups, medium (18 / 10 / 180) after 600, soft (0 / 0 / 0) after 1200 (cups of 40 ml).
