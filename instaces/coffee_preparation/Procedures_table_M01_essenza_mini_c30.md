### BLOCK 4 — Modeling notes

- Procedure dependencies (Preconditions column, "Requires procedure: <name>"): Descaling requires Turn machine ON; Emptying the system requires Turn machine ON; Turn machine OFF requires Turn machine ON. Turn machine ON postcondition (Coffee machine: Ready, LEDs: Steady) guarantees each dependent precondition (Coffee machine: Ready) and equals their Initial device state. Coffee preparation, First use and Programming the water volume keep switch-on as an inline step because the manual writes it as a step of those procedures.
- The same switch-on / switch-off transitions are reused inline (Coffee preparation step 1, First use steps 2 and 4, Programming the water volume step 1a, Disconnect step 1, Emptying the system step 1); the lever lift-and-close capsule ejection (Coffee preparation step 5b, Descaling step 1) is also a shared transition.
- Disconnect and the plug-in step of First use have low ecological validity: model Plug: Plugged in as a precondition with Disconnect / First use as resolver_procedure_id; Cleaning is excluded from the interactive build or used as resolver for Capsule container not full.
- Recommended primary EFSM: Coffee preparation (lever, capsule, cup, single button press; clean narrative). Programming the water volume, Energy saving mode, Reset to factory settings and the 5 s holds of Descaling and Emptying the system are hold interactions: second-order variants.
- Gesture overloading requires guards on Coffee machine mode and press duration: both buttons short press = Turn machine OFF; both buttons 5 s with machine ON = enter/exit Descaling mode; both buttons 5 s with machine Off, Water tank Removed and Lever Open = start Emptying the system; Espresso 5 s with machine Off = Energy saving mode; Lungo 5 s with machine Off = Reset to factory settings; Lungo 5 s in Energy-setting mode = exit.
- Explicit loops only: First use step 3 (x3); Descaling step 8 (repeat steps 4 and 7 once) and step 10 (repeat steps 4 and 7, repeat twice; interpreted as two rinse cycles, ambiguous). No other branches except Troubleshooting (decision by symptom, goto_procedure First use / Descaling, terminal escalation to Nespresso Club).
- Automatic timed transitions are not user actions: heat-up (~25 s), automatic stop of the coffee flow, Auto Off (9 or 30 min), flush until Water tank empty (Descaling step 7), automatic switch-off at the end of Emptying the system, LED confirmation sequences. The runtime must represent them as internal/timed events or simulated auto-advance.
- Error model: the device emits only LED patterns; Internal error paths combine Troubleshooting symptoms (observable) and Inferred guards from CAUTION/safeguard text (OnGuardFailed / OnUnexpectedAction), never device fault codes.
- Elements present in the full Component Table but absent from the supplied list (Cloth, Coffee grounds, Plastic film) are referenced only in Steps (plain) and Notes, never in component columns.
- Programming the water volume step 1c says only insert the capsule; lever open/close is inferred from Coffee preparation steps 2-3.
- Final state after exiting Energy saving mode and Water tank reinstallation after Emptying the system are not specified in manual.
- Manual inconsistencies: ES Descaling step 4 (0.8 l) and step 8 (steps 4 to 7); ES Energy saving step 1 (machine turning off); energy-saving icon artwork 3 min vs text 9 min.
