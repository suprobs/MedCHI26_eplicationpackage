## 7. Headset-free test (EfsmKeyboardTestDriver)
Add the component, Play, and follow the console key map in this order:
- Step 1: action `lift_lever` on `machine_lever` — "Lift the lever completely (manual step 1)."
- Step 2: action `close_lever` on `machine_lever` — "Close the lever to eject the capsule into the used capsule container (manual step 1)."
- Step 3: action `empty_drip_tray` on `machine_drip_tray` — "Empty the drip tray (manual step 2)."
- Step 4: action `empty_drip_base` on `machine_drip_base` — "Empty the drip base (manual step 2)."
- Step 5: action `empty_capsule_container` on `machine_capsule_container` — "Empty the used capsule container (manual step 2)."
- Step 6: action `pour_descaling_agent` on `machine_descaling_agent` — "Pour 1 Nespresso descaling liquid sachet into the water tank (manual step 3)."
- Step 7: action `fill_water_tank` on `machine_water_tank` — "Fill the water tank up to the 0.6 L / 20.3 oz line with potable water (manual step 3)."
- Step 8: action `place_container` on `machine_container` — "Place a container (min. 1 L / 33.8 oz) under the coffee outlet (manual step 4)."
- Step 9: action `hold_both_buttons` on `machine_leds` — "While the machine is turned on, press both the Espresso and Lungo buttons for 5 seconds to enter descaling mode; both LEDs blink (manual steps 5-6)."
- Step 10: action `press_lungo` on `machine_water_tank` — "Press the Lungo button and wait until the water tank is empty (manual step 7)."
- Step 11: action `remove_container` on `machine_container` — "Take the container with the used descaling solution from under the coffee outlet (implied by manual step 8)."
- Step 12: action `fill_water_tank` on `machine_water_tank` — "Refill the water tank with the used descaling solution collected in the container (manual step 8)."
- Step 13: action `place_container` on `machine_container` — "Place the container under the coffee outlet again (manual step 8: repeat step 4)."
- Step 14: action `press_lungo` on `machine_water_tank` — "Press the Lungo button and wait until the water tank is empty (manual step 8: repeat step 7)."
