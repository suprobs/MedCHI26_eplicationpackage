### BLOCK 4 — Cross-manual notes

- Single-manual session: no cross-manual comparison possible; all rows are [M01], 1/1.
- Display icons (Number of copies icon, Error icon, Paper Error icon, Wireless Attention icon, Signal Bars icon, Wi-Fi Direct icon, Ink Level icons) are extracted for fidelity but should be modeled as states of the single flat Control panel display machine, not as separate machines.
- Indicator lights (Power button light, Ink Alert light, Resume light, Wireless light, Information light) are observable effects; their causes are machine states (power, cartridge, error, wireless) and they are candidate ITS feedback anchors.
- Internal or service elements (Print carriage, Paper feed mechanism, Printhead, Printer contacts, Cartridge latch) are candidates for scene_props or excluded_components unless a target procedure makes them a transition subject; Print carriage is the observable effect of opening the Cartridge access door in Replace cartridges.
- This device emits real control panel error codes (E0 to E6) and ink and paper error statuses; these are device outputs. Rows tagged "inferred guard" or "CAUTION-derived guard" / "NOTE-derived guard" are safety conditions derived from the manual text, not device outputs.
- Cartridges are classified as material (replaceable consumable supplied by the user); Ink is an abstract_material candidate whose level is represented by the Ink Level icons; Plastic tape is a removable material that gates cartridge detection.
- Ethernet is listed as a supported connection type, but the back view shows only Power connection and USB port; no Ethernet port is extracted.
- Many control panel interactions are press-and-hold (3 s or 5 s) or two-button combinations (network setup, reports, Wi-Fi Direct); these are second-order variants for discrete MR modeling.
- Procedures driven only from HP Smart, printer software or EWS (scan settings, Quiet Mode, Sleep mode timing, align and clean cartridges, firmware update, Web Services) have no physical interaction beyond the material steps and are low ecological validity for the interactive build.
- Clear a paper jam from underneath the printer requires turning the whole printer over; low ecological validity for MR.
- Candidate discrete target procedures: Copy from the printer (Scanner lid, Scanner glass, Original, Start Copy Black or Color button), Load paper (Input tray, Paper-width guide, Output tray, Output tray extender), Replace cartridges (Cartridge access door, Cartridges, Plastic tape, Cartridge slot); printer powered on is a precondition.
- Physical dimensions not stated in the manual; layout hints can be read from the Printer overview figures (top and front view, back view) and the control panel figure (vertical strip, front-left).
