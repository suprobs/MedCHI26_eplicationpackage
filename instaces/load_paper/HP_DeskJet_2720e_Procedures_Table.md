### BLOCK 4 — Modeling notes

- Turning the printer on or off is not described as a standalone stepped procedure; Power button On/Off is modeled as a shared transition and as a precondition (printer on) for most procedures.
- Load paper and Load original are reused sub-steps of Copy from the printer, Scan, Print and maintenance procedures; model them as shared transitions or resolver procedures (preconditions with resolver_procedure_id), not repeated per procedure.
- Copy count via repeated presses of Start Copy Black button or Start Copy Color button is a parameterized transition on Number of copies icon (1 to 9), not a loop.
- Genuine branches (corrective actions that may or may not resolve): Fix cartridge problems, Improve print quality, all Clear a paper jam procedures (Cancel button first, manual clearing if it fails), Recover from paper size mismatch (E1, E2), Fix wireless connection, Fix Wi-Fi Direct connection. goto targets all exist as rows.
- Repetition: Replace cartridges repeats per cartridge (black, tri-color); Identify whether a cartridge has a problem is a two-pass test with one cartridge removed each pass.
- Software-only procedures (HP Smart, HP printer software, EWS, AirPrint) have no physical component transitions beyond Paper output; low ecological validity for the interactive MR build.
- Press-and-hold and two-button combinations (Wireless button with Cancel, Resume or Information button) are second-order hold interactions.
- Clear a paper jam from underneath the printer requires turning the whole device over; low ecological validity.
- Real device error outputs (E1 to E6, Paper Error icon, Ink Level icons states) drive the recovery procedures; CAUTION-derived conditions (unplug before moving Print carriage, never power off with Cartridges missing, cartridges out of printer under 30 minutes) are guards.
- Elements named by the manual but absent from the provided Component Table: signal bars (referenced via Control panel display), protective plastic tape (referenced as an action on Cartridges), dry swab or lint-free cloth (described, not mapped), index cards (not referenced).
- Candidate discrete EFSM targets: Copy from the printer, Load paper, Load original, Replace cartridges.
